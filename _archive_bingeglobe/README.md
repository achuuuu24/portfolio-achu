# BingeGlobe 🎬 — Global Web Series Directory & Review Portal

A sleek, responsive, and modern web application that provides comprehensive **information, reviews, ratings, and synopses/summaries** for iconic web series from around the globe (USA, UK, South Korea, Spain, Germany, India, Japan, and more).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Global Web Series Encyclopedia**:
   - Covers top-rated and critically acclaimed web series from multiple countries and languages (e.g., *Breaking Bad*, *Dark*, *Squid Game*, *Money Heist*, *Chernobyl*, *Succession*, *Stranger Things*, *Peaky Blinders*, *Severance*, *Sacred Games*, *The Boys*, *Alice in Borderland*, *Game of Thrones*, *The Last of Us*, *Black Mirror*, *Fleabag*, *Better Call Saul*, *Mirzapur*, *Crash Landing on You*, and *Narcos*).

2. **In-Depth Information for Every Series**:
   - **Story Synopsis / Small Summary**: Concise summary of the plot and premise.
   - **Critical Reviews & Verdicts**: Insightful breakdowns covering writing, acting, pacing, and direction.
   - **Strengths & Considerations (Pros / Cons)**: Clear highlights of what works and what viewers should know.
   - **Metadata**: Release year, seasons/episode counts, creator/showrunner, lead cast, IMDb & Rotten Tomatoes ratings, and streaming platforms (Netflix, HBO, Amazon Prime, Apple TV+, BBC).

3. **Smart Search & Multi-Filter System**:
   - **Real-Time Instant Search**: Filter by series name, actor, creator, plot keywords, or country.
   - **Genre Filtering**: Filter across Crime, Sci-Fi, Thriller, Mystery, Drama, Action, Comedy, Fantasy, Historical, and Romance.
   - **Origin Country Filter**: Explore titles from the USA, UK, South Korea, Spain, Germany, India, Japan, etc.
   - **Platform Badges**: One-click filters for Netflix, HBO, Prime Video, Apple TV+, and BBC.
   - **Sorting**: Sort by Highest Rated (IMDb), Newest Release, or Alphabetical (A-Z / Z-A).

4. **Interactive Features**:
   - **Detail Modal**: Click on any series card to open the cinematic modal with full plot, critical breakdown, and cast details.
   - **Add Your Own Series**: An interactive modal form to add any web series in the world to the catalog. Stored persistently using browser `localStorage`.
   - **Watchlist / Bookmarks**: Bookmark favorite series for quick tracking.
   - **Community Notes**: Leave personal reviews or thoughts on individual series.
   - **Global Statistics Counter**: Live updates showing total series, countries represented, average IMDb rating, and genres covered.
